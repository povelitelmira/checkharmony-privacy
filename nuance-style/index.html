Nuance Style Privacy Policy

Updated: 27 November 2026.
Developer: Andrei Khadatchuk.
Privacy and support: checkflow.support@gmail.com.

Nuance Style helps you organise your wardrobe, create outfits and explore colours and clothing shapes. Your wardrobe, photos and analysis results are processed and stored on your device. The app has no user accounts, advertising or developer-operated cloud synchronisation. Google SDKs can process technical diagnostics as explained below.

Information used on your device

You may provide a name for the greeting, quiz answers, body measurements, photos for style analysis, clothing photos and item details. The app saves analysis results, palettes, wardrobe and Wish list items, outfits, photo adjustments, language and other preferences locally. Analysis history holds up to five records; older records are replaced when you save additional analyses.

Photo analysis supports colour and style suggestions. It is not used to identify or authenticate you. You can use the wardrobe without completing the style quizzes.

Photos and camera access

The camera is used when you choose to take a photo or scan a transfer QR code. The photo picker opens images you select. Nuance Style may create local copies, resized images, background cutouts and outfit previews. The originals in your phone’s gallery are not deleted when you remove an item from the app. You can refuse camera permission or revoke it in Android settings.

Google ML Kit and internet connections

Google ML Kit supports functions including background removal and fallback text translation. Google states that ML Kit processes input images and text on the device and does not send these inputs or analysis outputs to Google servers. Some features need an internet connection to download models or updates before they can work offline.

ML Kit collects technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics. This can include device and app details, installation or device identifiers depending on the SDK, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes, operational events and error codes. Translation can include the configured source and destination languages. Google documents HTTPS transmission and the use of Firebase services for translation configuration diagnostics. These records are distinct from your clothing photos and quiz contents.

See ML Kit data disclosures, ML Kit privacy terms and Google’s Privacy Policy. Nuance Style does not operate its own advertising or behavioural analytics service.

Manual transfer between phones

You can transfer your name, results, wardrobe, Wish list, outfits, settings and associated photos over a local Wi-Fi connection. Transfer starts at your request, uses encryption and requires confirmation before replacing data on the receiving phone. The old phone retains its data. The QR code contains access details for the transfer; show it only to your own receiving device.

The current Android app is configured to exclude app data from automatic Android cloud backup and system device transfer. Nuance Style’s manual QR transfer remains available. The app does not provide an online account from which the developer can restore your wardrobe.

Sharing and external services

If you share an outfit image, Android passes it to the app or recipient you choose. Pinterest searches open an external service with a clothing-type and colour query. Opening a privacy or other external link also connects you to that website. External services apply their own privacy policies; Nuance Style does not automatically upload your wardrobe photos to Pinterest.

The optional home-screen widget displays general style tips and uses your selected language. Its contents are available to the launcher displaying it.

Retention, deletion and security

Local data stays in the app until you remove or replace it. Use the app’s controls to delete individual analyses, wardrobe items or outfits. To remove all local app records, photos and preferences, use Android’s Clear storage option or uninstall Nuance Style. Copies you shared, original gallery photos and data transferred to another phone must be deleted separately.

Android’s app storage isolation protects local records from ordinary access by other apps. Local storage is not a promise of separate password encryption for every file. Use your device’s screen lock and security updates. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete data held only on your phone.

Support and privacy requests

If you email checkflow.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email uses Gmail and is subject to Google’s policies. Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may use the same address to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to the developer. Do not send private photos or wardrobe exports unless they are necessary for your request.

Policy updates and this website

This page will be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The revision date appears above. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of website visitor information. This page has no added analytics scripts or advertising.
