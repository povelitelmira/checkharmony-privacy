CheckHarmony Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Developer: Andrei Khadatchuk. App and privacy contact: checkflow.support@gmail.com.

App records

CheckHarmony helps you capture tasks, organise lists and projects, plan activities and receive reminders. A CheckHarmony account is not required. The app contains no advertising.

Tasks, notes, projects, lists, dates, completion history, settings and voice notes are stored in the app’s storage on your device. They support planning, progress displays, widgets and reminders. CheckHarmony does not provide its own cloud synchronisation of these records.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone access supports voice notes and dictation. Voice notes are stored on your device and may be included in exported backups.

By default, CheckHarmony requests on-device speech recognition. This requires support from the device and a language model. Android may use an internet connection to download that model.

If you turn off the on-device-only option, an external speech service may process dictation. Audio and related technical request data may be sent to that service provider. Processing depends on the speech service installed on your phone and its privacy policy. You can keep local recognition enabled or type instead.

Camera and QR codes

The camera reads project QR codes. Scanning takes place on the device and the recognised project is imported into CheckHarmony. The Android version uses Google ML Kit.

Google documents that ML Kit collects technical device and app details, installation identifiers, operational events and performance information for diagnostics and usage analytics, using HTTPS for transmission. These technical records are distinct from the contents of your tasks and projects.

Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Reminders and widgets

Android schedules notifications. Notification and exact alarm permissions may be required. Task titles and times may appear on your lock screen according to your phone’s settings.

When you add a CheckHarmony widget, selected task information appears on your home screen and is available to the launcher hosting the widget. Configure lock-screen and widget visibility to suit the sensitivity of your records.

Sharing, clipboard and backups

Copying places text on the system clipboard. When sharing text, projects or backups, you choose the receiving app and recipient. The receiving service processes data under its own rules.

Backups may include text, dates, settings and voice notes. Exported files do not have CheckHarmony password encryption. Store them securely and share them only with trusted recipients. The app lock does not encrypt exported backups.

Support requests

When you email checkflow.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send, to respond and investigate the issue. Support email uses Gmail and is also subject to Google’s policies. Avoid sending sensitive records or backups unless necessary.

Support correspondence is retained for up to 12 months after the request is resolved, then deleted unless longer retention is required by law. Privacy requests can be sent to the support address above.

Retention, deletion and security

Local records remain until you delete them. Deleted entries may remain in Trash for up to 30 days; you can empty Trash manually. The Delete all data action clears app records. Android’s Clear storage action or uninstalling removes the app’s local storage, including settings. You must separately delete any copies you previously exported or shared.

App lock restricts access through CheckHarmony’s interface. It does not mean the entire task database or exported files are encrypted. Protect your device with a secure screen lock and keep Android updated.

For access, correction or deletion requests concerning data you sent to the developer, contact checkflow.support@gmail.com. The developer cannot recover or delete records held only on your device and never sent to the developer. You can also contact external providers about data they process.

Updates

When features or data practices change, this policy will be updated on this page. The date of the latest update appears at the beginning of the document.
